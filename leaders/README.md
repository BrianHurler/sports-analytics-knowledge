# Sports Analytics Leaders

This section studies influential sports-analytics practitioners and researchers through the methods they developed, the questions they asked, and the way their work connects across the seven-pillar framework.

The purpose is not biography for its own sake. Each profile is intended to answer three questions:

1. **What analytical problems did this person help make tractable?**
2. **What methods or mental models are worth learning from their work?**
3. **How does the work connect to the seven pillars of this repository?**

## Profiles

- [Luke Bornn](luke-bornn.md) — player tracking, spatial analytics, expected possession value, Bayesian models, metric evaluation, forecasting, decision processes, and applications across basketball, soccer, and hockey.
- [Eric Tulsky](eric-tulsky.md) — hockey measurement design, contextual player evaluation, repeatability, zone-entry and neutral-zone analytics, state decomposition, draft-market analysis, and decision-focused research.
- [Sunny Mehta](sunny-mehta.md) — measurement bias, contextual expectation, signal-versus-noise thinking, player projection, expected value, risk, optionality, and front-office decision-making under uncertainty.

## Profile template

Each leader page should include:

- a short explanation of why the person matters analytically
- recurring themes in their work
- a research inventory, prioritizing publicly available sports-analytics work
- a short practical summary of each paper/project
- explicit links to the relevant seven pillars
- a synthesis of what an applied analyst should learn from the body of work

The goal is to turn a publication list into a structured learning path.
