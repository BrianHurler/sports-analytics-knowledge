# Pillar 6: Decision Science

## Central question

**What should we do?**

Decision science combines estimates, uncertainty, constraints, and objectives to recommend actions rather than merely describe or predict outcomes.

## Core ideas

- simulation
- Monte Carlo methods
- optimization
- expected utility
- constraints
- scenario analysis
- risk and uncertainty
- sensitivity analysis

## Sports examples

Beach volleyball:
- how much a tactical change alters match-win probability
- what serve strategy maximizes expected rally value against a specific opponent
- which skill improvement would most improve match outcomes

Ice hockey:
- lineup and roster optimization
- acquisition scenarios under salary-cap constraints
- tactical choices by game state

## Explain → Build → Apply → Defend

**Explain:** Distinguish prediction from action selection and explain the objective being optimized.

**Build:** Create a basic simulator or constrained optimization model.

**Apply:** Use it to compare realistic sports decisions under uncertainty.

**Defend:** Justify the objective, constraints, input models, uncertainty assumptions, sensitivity, and practical feasibility of the recommendation.

## Key warning

An optimizer will confidently optimize the wrong objective if the objective or constraints are poorly specified.
