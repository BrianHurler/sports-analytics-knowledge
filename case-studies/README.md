# Case Studies

Case studies connect concepts to complete analytical reasoning. They are not production analyses and should not contain sensitive or proprietary implementation details.

Each case study should follow this structure:

1. Question
2. Why the naive answer is insufficient
3. Data available
4. Estimand or prediction target
5. Candidate approaches
6. Chosen method
7. Assumptions
8. Validation
9. Result interpretation
10. Decision implication
11. What could still be wrong?
12. What would productionization look like?

The goal is to practice the full chain from sports question to analytical judgment.
